01 — About

Automation should keep working after the demo.

I build practical AI automation around real business processes: lead handling, content operations, alerts, data pipelines and customer conversations.

n8n is the orchestration layer. AI models handle judgment and language. APIs connect the system to Google Sheets, Gmail, Slack, Supabase, social platforms and the rest of the stack.

The goal is never “AI for AI's sake.” The goal is less manual work, faster response times and a workflow someone else can understand when it is handed over.
